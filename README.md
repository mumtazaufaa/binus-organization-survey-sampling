# Student Organization Participation Survey Analysis

A survey and sampling methods project examining the relationship between student organization participation and students' perceived career readiness, networking development, and skill development at BINUS University.

The study uses primary survey data collected through an online questionnaire and applies **Proportionate Stratified Random Sampling** to BINUS students from the 2026 and 2027 cohorts.

> **Scope note:** The analysis is based on self-reported survey responses and describes associations and perceptions within the surveyed population. The reported relationships should not be interpreted as causal effects.

## Research Focus

The project investigates three main questions:

1. What is the relationship between organizational participation and BINUS students' readiness for the workplace?
2. What is the relationship between organizational participation and students' ability to build professional networks?
3. What is the relationship between organizational participation and students' skill development?

## Methodology

### Data Collection

The project uses **primary data** collected directly from respondents through an online questionnaire.

The questionnaire was distributed using Google Forms over approximately 2–3 weeks.

Before the main survey, a pre-test was conducted with **30 students** to evaluate whether the questions were clear, relevant, and consistent with the research topic.

### Population and Sample

The population is BINUS University students.

The study focuses on students from:

- BINUSIAN 2026
- BINUSIAN 2027

The report states a population size of **20,867 students** for these cohorts.

### Sampling Technique

The project uses:

```text
Proportionate Stratified Random Sampling
```

The population is divided into strata, with the report describing the strata as approximately 30% each.

## Variables

### Independent Variables

- Duration of organizational participation
- Leadership experience
- Skill development associated with organizational participation

### Dependent Variables

- Workplace readiness
- Networking development
- Application of organizational skills in the workplace
- Identification and improvement of personal weaknesses

## Questionnaire Design

The questionnaire consists of four main sections.

### Section 1 — Respondent Information

Includes:

- Study program
- BINUSIAN cohort
- Organizational participation status

### Section 2 — Organizational Experience

Includes:

- Organization name
- Leadership experience
- Duration of organizational participation

Duration categories:

- `< 6 months`
- `6–12 months`
- `> 12 months`

### Section 3 — Skill Development

Respondents rate the perceived importance of organizational participation in developing:

- Communication
- Leadership
- Teamwork
- Problem solving
- Time management
- Technical skills
- Confidence
- Adaptability

All items use a 1–5 scale.

### Section 4 — Career and Professional Development

Respondents evaluate whether organizational participation helps them:

- Prepare for the workplace
- Build professional networks
- Apply acquired skills in the workplace
- Address personal weaknesses
- Identify personal weaknesses

## Survey Results

The survey received **367 responses**.

The report identifies:

| Response Group | Count | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Valid ("Yes") | 305 | 88.3% |
| Invalid ("No") | 62 | 16.7% |

The percentages reported in the source report are preserved as submitted.

## Validity and Reliability

The analysis was performed using the **305 valid respondents**.

Reported metrics:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Average inter-item correlation | 0.57 |
| Total variance | 25.96 |
| Variance of total scores | 107.44 |
| Cronbach's Alpha | 0.82 |

The report interprets the Cronbach's Alpha value of **0.82** as indicating strong internal consistency for the survey instrument.

## Findings: Workplace Readiness

The report examines perceived relationships between organizational participation and workplace-related skills.

| Skill | Reported Correlation | Respondents Reporting Improvement |
|---|---:|---:|
| Adaptability | 0.62 | 87% |
| Problem Solving | 0.59 | 80% |
| Communication | 0.57 | 85% |
| Time Management | 0.54 | 79% |

The corresponding average scores reported by the project are:

| Skill | Average Score |
|---|---:|
| Adaptability | 4.5 |
| Problem Solving | 4.3 |
| Communication | 4.4 |
| Time Management | 4.2 |

The report identifies adaptability as the highest reported correlation among these workplace-readiness dimensions.

## Findings: Networking

The survey also evaluates perceived networking benefits.

| Networking Aspect | Respondents | Average Score |
|---|---:|---:|
| Networking benefits | 78% | 4.3 |
| Leadership experience | 75% | 4.2 |
| External collaboration | 80% | 4.4 |

The report describes organizational activities such as seminars, workshops, inter-organization collaboration, competitions, and external events as contexts in which students can develop networking opportunities.

## Findings: Skill Development

The report presents the following relationships between organizational participation and selected skills:

| Skill | Reported Correlation | Respondents Reporting Improvement |
|---|---:|---:|
| Leadership | 0.57 | 82% |
| Communication | 0.57 | 85% |
| Teamwork | 0.56 | 83% |
| Adaptability | 0.62 | 87% |

The report identifies adaptability as the highest correlation among the four listed skills.
